# Sales-Analysis-Dashboard-Tableau- Retail Sales Dashboard

An interactive Tableau dashboard for exploring retail sales performance across time, product categories, regions, states, and sales channels. It also highlights profitability, order status, and the top products by sales.

## Dashboard contents

The workbook includes a main dashboard with:

- KPI summary
- Monthly sales trend
- Sales by category and profit by category
- Sales by state and region
- Sales by channel
- Top 10 products by sales

Additional worksheets include month-over-month sales growth and order status.

## Data

The workbook uses the `Retail Sales` table from an Excel source named `tableau_retail_sales_1800_rows.xlsx`. The fields include order date, customer and segment, geography, sales channel, category and product, quantity, unit price, discount, sales, profit, shipping cost, payment and shipping modes, and order status. Monetary values are in Indian rupees (INR).
